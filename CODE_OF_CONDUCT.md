# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Contributors and maintainers must provide a professional, harassment-free
environment regardless of background, identity, experience, nationality,
appearance, disability, religion, or technical preference.

Expected conduct includes constructive disagreement, evidence-based review,
respect for privacy, clear boundaries, and accountability for mistakes.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, doxxing, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, retaliation, publishing private information,
or using project access to pressure another participant.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or revoke access
when conduct threatens contributors or the project. A responder with a conflict
must recuse. Two people should review enforcement decisions when two eligible
responders are available.

A monitored private conduct-reporting address has not yet been published.
Until it exists, do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in public issues.
Repository administrators must establish the private channel before broad
community intake. Security advisories are not a conduct-reporting channel.
