# Conformance Policy

Conformance results identify:

- implementation and version;
- specification and profile;
- conformance-suite revision;
- platform;
- signed result and expiry.

The public suite contains positive, negative, boundary, adversarial,
cross-language, compatibility, crash, and resource-exhaustion cases as the
relevant surfaces mature.

Conformance establishes compatibility with named protocol behavior. It does
not certify:

- security;
- evidence truth;
- evaluator or policy quality;
- regulatory compliance;
- financial fitness;
- strategy safety or profitability.

Results expire after an incompatible profile release and may be revoked if the
submitted implementation or evidence was misrepresented.
